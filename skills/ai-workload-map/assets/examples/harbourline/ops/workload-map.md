# AI workload map: Harbourline Cycles marketing

2026-10-20 · built from team/tasks.csv, team/tools.md and brand/rules.md · Draft (brand memory has rules only)

## Recommendation

Start with the Monday sales summary and the subscriber tagging: both are internal, repeat every week and are easy to check. Stop faxing price lists once both partners confirm email. Keep the flyer print approval and warranty replies with a person. Product descriptions and the newsletter move to AI-assisted drafting in month two, with Amelia approving each. About 4.5 hours a week could come back to the team (an estimate from assumed savings rates, below).

## Risks and guardrails

- Unsupported price claims in drafts: the brand rule says never "best prices in Halifax". Amelia checks every draft for it.
- Wrong figures to the owners: Luis checks the summary totals against Shopify for the first four Mondays.
- Subscriber data: the tagging agent edits tags only; no exports, no deletes, no shared logins.
- Spelling drift: Canadian spelling in every draft (brand rule).
