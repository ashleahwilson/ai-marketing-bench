# Ferncliff marketing job descriptions

Ferncliff makes online scheduling and reminder software for dental clinics in the US.

## Marketing Manager (Rosa Delgado)

- Owns the marketing plan and the quarterly budget ($45,000 a quarter), and approves every spend over $1,000
- Runs the monthly webinar for clinic office managers, including picking topics and booking guest dentists
- Writes the monthly customer newsletter
- Approves all customer-facing content before it goes out
- Manages the relationship with the Midwest Dental Association, our largest event partner
- Reviews and signs vendor and sponsorship contracts with the COO

## Marketing Coordinator (Tyler Benn)

- Builds the webinar registration page and sends the reminder and follow-up emails
- Pulls the monthly marketing metrics (webinar registrations, demo requests, newsletter opens) into a slide for the leadership meeting
- Posts three times a week on LinkedIn and Facebook
- Replies to comments on our social posts
- Updates customer logos and testimonials on the website
- Ships booth materials for two trade shows a year
