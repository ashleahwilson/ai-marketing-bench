# How we do things (marketing)

## Webinars
Rosa picks the topic and the guest. Tyler builds the registration page in WordPress, sends three reminder emails from HubSpot, runs the Zoom, and sends the recording to registrants the next day. Tyler also writes a summary blog post from the recording, usually a week later.

## Monthly metrics
Tyler exports numbers from HubSpot and Zoom into a spreadsheet, then copies them into the leadership slide. It takes most of a day each month and the slide format never changes.

## Complaints forwarded by clinics
Sometimes a clinic forwards us a complaint from one of their patients about a missed reminder, with the patient's name, phone number and appointment details. Tyler logs it in a spreadsheet and sends it to support. Rosa replies to the clinic.

## Testimonials
Tyler collects quotes from clinic customers by email. Rosa checks the clinic has agreed in writing before a quote goes on the website.

## Trade shows
Tyler orders and ships the booth kit and the brochures. Brochures are reprinted for every show (about 800 copies); we usually bring about 600 home.
