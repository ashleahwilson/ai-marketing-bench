# Meta ads audit: Harbourline Kayak Co.

2026-08-01 to 2026-08-31 (31 days) · CAD · audited 2026-09-03
**Score:** 74/100 (Sound) · **Confidence:** High (31 days, 107 conversions) · **Compliance:** Clear
**Status:** provisional (no brand memory: goal assumed to be online kayak and gear purchases)

**Recommendation:** Refresh the cart retargeting ad, which has worn out, and fold the camping interest ad set into the Advantage+ ad set before adding budget.

## Top three findings
1. **The cart retargeting ad has worn out.** RT | Add to cart 14d ran one ad at frequency 9.4. Between the first and second half of August its CTR fell from 2.01% to 1.21% and its cost per purchase rose from $14.15 to $97.55. **Fix:** add two new ads in different formats and cap the audience window. (F1)
2. **The camping interest ad set is too small to learn and costs the most.** Kayaks | Interest: camping took $930.00 for 9 purchases (2.03 a week, against Meta's guideline of about 50 optimization events per ad set per week), $103.33 per purchase against $54.71 in Kayaks | Advantage+ audience. **Fix:** pause it and move its budget to the Advantage+ ad set. (F2)
3. **Too few ads to test.** 4 ads across three ad sets; two ad sets run a single image ad, and only one ad is a video. **Fix:** three to four ads per prospecting ad set across video, image and carousel. (F3)

## Plan
### Next 48 hours
- Add two new ads to RT | Add to cart 14d (F1).
- Pause Kayaks | Interest: camping and move its daily budget to Kayaks | Advantage+ audience (F2).
### Next 2 weeks
- Add video and carousel ads to the prospecting ad set (F3).
- Send the Audience Overlap export and the ad set exclusions so the overlap check can run.
### Next 3 months
- Rotate new creative into retargeting monthly; watch frequency and CTR in the day breakdown.
- Compare Meta's purchase count with the store's each month (August: 107 attributed by Meta, 164 orders from all sources).
