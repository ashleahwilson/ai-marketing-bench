Subject: Your sales tax filings are already covered

Quick reminder for anyone still paying a separate firm to file sales tax: you don't need to. Every Tallyhouse plan includes sales tax filings, and your bookkeeper prepares and files them each period.

A few clients told us this month they'd been paying twice without realizing it. If that's you, you can stop.

To hand it over, send your bookkeeper last quarter's notices from the tax authority. A photo or PDF is fine. They'll check whether anything's outstanding and take it from there.

If you're not sure what you've been sent, reply to this email with whatever notices you have, and your bookkeeper will sort out what matters.
