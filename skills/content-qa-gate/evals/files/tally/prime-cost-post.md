# How to read your prime cost

Prime cost is your food, beverage and labour cost added together, divided by your sales. It's the number that tells you whether last month worked.

Restaurants that track prime cost weekly are 30% more profitable than those that don't. Most owners only find out what it was weeks after the month ends, when the books finally close.

![](prime-cost-chart.png)

# Why it matters

If your prime cost runs above about 65% of sales, there's not much left for rent, repairs and you. The fix is almost always the same: see the number sooner, so you can act on on it while the month is still running.
