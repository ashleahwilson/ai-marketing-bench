# Seamless Sync: your Shopify revenue dashboard, refreshed every 15 minutes

As a Shopify partner, we've always wanted merchants to see revenue sooner. Starting today, Seamless Sync pulls your Shopify and Stripe data into one dashboard every 15 minutes, so you're looking at real-time revenue instead of yesterday's.

It's free on every plan. To turn it on, go to Settings > Integrations and switch on Seamless Sync.

## Pair it with inventory data

Revenue is half the picture. We recommend [Stockline](https://stockline.example/?ref=quill) for inventory: it connects to Shopify in a few minutes, and with both running you can see which products are selling and which are about to run out.
