About Ferncliff

Priya Natarajan ran the front desk at her father's dental clinic for six years. Every morning started with the same job: phoning the waitlist to fill the day's cancellations. She started Ferncliff in 2022 to hand that job to software.

We build one thing: scheduling for independent dental clinics with 1 to 5 locations. We're a team of 9 in Columbus, Ohio.
