About Quillmetrics

Quillmetrics builds one revenue dashboard for Shopify merchants doing $1M to $20M a year. It combines your Shopify and Stripe data, so you see revenue, refunds and subscription renewals in one place instead of two.

Our Seamless Sync integration refreshes both every 15 minutes.

We started in 2023 after running finance for two Shopify stores. Today we're a team of 7 in Austin, Texas.
