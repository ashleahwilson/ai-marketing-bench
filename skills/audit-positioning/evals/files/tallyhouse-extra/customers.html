Lantern Noodle Bar, 3 locations, Toronto

Before Tallyhouse, Lantern's books for a month were usually closed around the 20th of the next month. Since moving to Tallyhouse in March 2026, every month has closed by the 3rd. September's books closed on October 3.

"We finally know our prime cost before the month is over." — Maria Okafor, owner, Lantern Noodle Bar (3 locations)
