How Pipewise works

Every morning at 8, Pipewise ranks your team's open deals by how likely each one is to close this month, using the emails, meetings and stage changes already in your CRM.

Your reps open it at 9 and know which deal to call first. Your forecast is the sum of those rankings, updated daily, so the Monday forecast call starts from numbers instead of guesses.

Built for B2B software sales teams of 10 to 100 reps.
