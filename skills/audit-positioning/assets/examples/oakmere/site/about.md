# About Oakmere

Oakmere is payroll built for independent veterinary clinics in Ontario and British Columbia. It calculates on-call pay, overtime for technicians, and relief vet contracts automatically, the parts general payroll software leaves to a spreadsheet.

Founded in 2024 by Lena Forsythe, who managed the books at a three-vet clinic in Guelph. We're a team of 5.
