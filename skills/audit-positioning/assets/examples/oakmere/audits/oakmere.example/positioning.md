# Positioning: Oakmere
2026-10-22 · B2B software (payroll) · Founder-led · Pages reviewed: index, pricing, about (copy) · Brand memory: none, so this is provisional · Competitor pages: none

## Verdict
40/100 on the positioning checklist · The about page already has the position: move it to the home page and drop the 5-minute line until you can show it.

## The three findings that matter most
1. **The home page hides what the about page says plainly.** Evidence, index: "Payroll that just works". About: "Oakmere is payroll built for independent veterinary clinics in Ontario and British Columbia." Fix: put the about page's first sentence in the H1 this week.
2. **The headline could belong to any payroll company.** Evidence, index: "Payroll that just works" and "takes the stress out of payroll so you can focus on what matters". Neither names a buyer, a problem or anything only Oakmere does. Fix: lead with on-call pay and relief vet contracts (headline options below).
3. **"Run payroll in 5 minutes" has nothing behind it.** Evidence, index subhead. No page shows a measured pay run, and the only testimonial is about on-call pay, not speed. Fix: remove the line, or replace it with one client's timed pay run, named.

## Who it's for, and who it's not
Specific, but only on the about page: "independent veterinary clinics in Ontario and British Columbia". The home and pricing pages never name a buyer. No exclusions anywhere (multi-clinic groups? clinics outside those provinces?). Fix: add the audience to the home subhead and one "not for" line.

## Category frame
About: "payroll built for independent veterinary clinics" is a clear shelf. Home: "Payroll that just works" puts Oakmere on the general payroll shelf, next to every alternative it's meant to beat.

## Problem and the alternative
The about page names both: "on-call pay, overtime for technicians, and relief vet contracts", "the parts general payroll software leaves to a spreadsheet". That's a real contrast. It's absent from the home page.

## Differentiated value and proof
The difference is specific and supported: automatic on-call and relief vet pay (about, pricing), backed by Dr. Sam Okoye, Riverbend Animal Hospital: "On-call pay used to take me a full evening every pay run." The unsupported line is the 5-minute claim.

## Specificity and the swap test
- "Payroll that just works": generic, any payroll company could say it.
- "takes the stress out of payroll so you can focus on what matters": generic.
- "Easy setup": generic.
- "calculates on-call pay, overtime for technicians, and relief vet contracts automatically": specific.
- Named swap test: not run, no competitor pages supplied. Send the home pages of the two payroll providers you lose to most to run it.

## Headline options
**A.** Headline: "Payroll for vet clinics, on-call pay included" · Subhead: "Oakmere calculates on-call pay, technician overtime and relief vet contracts automatically, for independent clinics in Ontario and British Columbia."
Built from: about ("on-call pay, overtime for technicians, and relief vet contracts automatically"; "independent veterinary clinics in Ontario and British Columbia")
**B.** Headline: "Your on-call pay, already calculated" · Subhead: "Payroll built for independent veterinary clinics. The parts general payroll software leaves to a spreadsheet, done for you."
Built from: index testimonial ("already calculated when I open Oakmere"); about ("the parts general payroll software leaves to a spreadsheet")

Recommend A: it names the category, the buyer and the difference in one screen.

## Plan
