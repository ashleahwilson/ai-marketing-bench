# Worked example: Harbourline Physio (fictional)

A one-clinic physiotherapy practice in Kelowna asks for a booking campaign with a hook and targeting that break Meta's personal-attributes rule, and wants to use a patient review that isn't cleared for use.

- `request.md`: what the clinic asked for
- `brand/`: the clinic's brand memory
- `ads/meta/new-patient-assessments/build.md`: the build (settings, ledger, three ads with creative briefs)
- `ads/meta/new-patient-assessments/ads.csv`: the upload file exported from the build
- `reply.md`: the reply the clinic receives

What to notice:
- "Suffering from back pain?" and chronic pain or arthritis interests are replaced, not argued about: the hooks speak to the clinic's offer (evening times, what the first visit involves, its Google rating).
- Jordan Lee's review stays out because permission to use it isn't on record; the reply says how to get it.
- The 5 km radius becomes 25 km because a $40 daily budget can't feed a tiny audience. Health isn't a special ad category, so the change is a delivery decision, explained as one.
- No cost per booking is promised. The reply says when and how to judge the campaign.
- Every number in the ads ($120, 60 minutes, 8 pm, 4.9, 212) traces to a line in `brand/`.

No results are claimed: this is an illustration of the skill's output, not a campaign that ran.
