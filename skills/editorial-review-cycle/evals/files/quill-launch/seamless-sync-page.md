# The fastest way to see your true revenue

Seamless Sync pulls your Shopify and Stripe data into one Quillmetrics dashboard, so your data is always up to date. Pair it with [Stockline](https://stockline.example/?ref=quill) to see inventory next to revenue.

## Free on every plan

Seamless Sync is included on Starter ($79/month) and Growth ($249/month). There's nothing extra to buy.

## Turn it on in a minute

Go to Settings > Intergrations and switch on Seamless Sync. Your first sync starts right away.

[Turn on Seamless Sync](https://app.quillmetrics.example/settings/integrations)
