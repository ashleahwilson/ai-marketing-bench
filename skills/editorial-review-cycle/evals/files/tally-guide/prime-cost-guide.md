# How to read your prime cost before the month is over

Prime cost is your food and beverage cost plus your total labour cost, divided by sales for the same period. It's the one number that tells you whether last month worked.

Prime cost matters because it adds up your two biggest costs. When you know it, you know whether last month worked, and that's why it's the number to watch.

## Why Prime Cost Matters A Lot

Most owners only see their prime cost weeks after the end of the month, when the schedule and the orders are already locked in. By then you can't change anything. Seeing it early means you can fix next week's schedule or a menu price while it still counts.

A useful target for food cost is 28–32% of food sales.
