# How automatic reminders change patient behavior and fill your schedule, and why your front desk will thank you

Every empty chair costs your clinic money. When a patient forgets an appointment, the hygienist's hour is gone and the front desk spends the afternoon calling the waitlist.

Automatic reminders change patient behavior. Patients who get a text before their visit are more likely to show up, confirm, or reschedule early enough for you to fill the slot.

Ferncliff sends every patient a text reminder 2 hours before their appointment. It eliminates no-shows, so your team can stop chasing confirmations by phone.

You'll recieve a color-coded view of the day, so the front desk can see at a glance who has confirmed and who hasn't. Use it to optimize your schedule before the day starts.
