# What your labour % is telling you

Labour % is your total labour cost, including payroll taxes and benefits, divided by your sales for the same period. If you paid $18,000 in labour on $60,000 of sales, your labour % is 30%.

The number only helps if you see it while you can still change next week's schedule. The industry standard is that labour should be 25–30% of sales.

## Where to see it

Food cost and labour % sit on one dashboard, next to your daily sales from Toast, Square or Lightspeed. Your bookkeeper, who has worked in hospitality finance, closes the month and sends your P&L.
