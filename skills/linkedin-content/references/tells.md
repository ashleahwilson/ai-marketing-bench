# LinkedIn AI tells

Patterns readers now recognise as generated or as growth-hack formula. None of them goes in a post, even when the user's draft has it or the user asks for it. Each has a replacement that keeps what the user wanted.

## Broetry

One-sentence lines stacked with blank lines between them, so a 60-word thought fills a phone screen.

> That's too late.
>
> Way too late.
>
> And it's costing you.

**Instead:** join them into a paragraph of two to four sentences. One short line on its own is allowed once per post, for weight. Lists of real items (checklist steps) don't count.

## Engagement bait

Asking for a reaction instead of earning one: "Agree?", "Thoughts?", "Comment YES and I'll send it", "Comment CHECKLIST", "Tag someone who needs this", "Repost if you agree", "Follow for more", "Who else…?", "Drop a 🔥 below", "♻️ Repost".

**Instead:**
- To hand out a resource: a link in the post or first comment, or "message me and I'll send it". If there's no link yet, mark `[add link]` in the first comment and list it. Never invent one.
- To start a conversation, only when the user asks for one: a specific question the person would want answered ("What do you check every Monday?"). Otherwise end on the practice or the next step.
- To grow followers: nothing. A post worth reading is the follow button.

If the user asked for bait, give the clean version and say in one or two sentences why (it reads as a formula and costs trust with the people they want to reach; a real link or question does the same job). Don't lecture, and don't refuse the post.

## Hashtag piles

More hashtags than the brand allows, or generic ones (#success #motivation #business). **Instead:** the brand's limit, or at most three specific tags at the end when there's no rule. Zero is fine.

## Performed candour

Announcing honesty or vulnerability instead of showing it: "I'll be honest", "Unpopular opinion", "Hot take", "Let that sink in", "Read that again", "I almost didn't post this", "Nobody talks about this", "Here's the thing", "I'm humbled to announce", "I was wrong about…" (when the material doesn't say so).

**Instead:** say the thing. If it's honest, it reads as honest.

## Manufactured stories and friction

A story, struggle or turning point the material doesn't contain: "The founder was about to give up", "I almost quit", "Then it hit me", "Here's what happened next", "Plot twist", an invented client, an invented dialogue, a 10x result nobody measured. Also invented tension: "Everyone told me I was crazy".

**Instead:** use the real story from the material, with its real detail. If the user wants a story and hasn't supplied one, ask for it with three or four questions they can answer in a minute (what happened, when, what changed, how they know). Offer a post you can write now from what's real.

## Population openers

Opening with a claim about a whole group: "Most leaders…", "Every founder…", "Too many teams…", "Most dental offices lose a fortune…". It's unprovable and it's the opener of a thousand other posts.

**Instead:** open with the specific case in the material (the pilot clinic, the four-location group, the 40 owners in the room).

## Other stock moves

- "Not X, but Y" / "It's not about X. It's about Y."
- Lists of lessons the person didn't draw ("Here are 5 lessons I learned…").
- Emoji bullets and arrows (→ ✅ 🚀) used as decoration.
- Stock words: journey, landscape, game-changer, unlock, elevate, empower, leverage, navigate, seamless (unless it's a product name), thought leader, move the needle.
- Em dashes used as the default joint. Use commas, colons or full stops.
- A closing summary that repeats the post.
