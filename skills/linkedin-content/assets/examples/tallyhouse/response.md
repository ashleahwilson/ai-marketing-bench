Last month an owner came to us seven months behind on their books, with the receipts in a shoebox.

The owners who come to us behind are embarrassed. They shouldn't be. Nobody opens a restaurant to do bookkeeping.

I didn't start with the shoebox. I started with the bank and card statements, because the bank already has every transaction. Then the POS sales reports, then the invoices. The receipts come last, only to fill the gaps.

If you're behind, that order works whether you do it yourself or hand it to a bookkeeper. And if you hand it to us, catch-up is quoted per month of backlog, so you know the cost before we start.
