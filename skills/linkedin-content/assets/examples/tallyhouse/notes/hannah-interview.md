# Interview notes: Hannah Ruiz, Oct 21 (for a LinkedIn post)

Topic: owners who are months behind on their books.

- Hannah: "The owners who come to us behind are embarrassed. They shouldn't be. Nobody opens a restaurant to do bookkeeping."
- Last month an owner came in seven months behind, with receipts in a shoebox. Rosa, from Pico's Taqueria. Hannah started with the bank statements, not the receipts, because the bank has every transaction already.
- Her order: bank and card statements first, then POS sales reports, then invoices. Receipts last, only to fill gaps.
- How we price it: catch-up is quoted per month of backlog, so you know the cost before we start.
- Hannah said we can get anyone caught up in 3 days. (Dev: careful with that one.)
- She thinks about a third of new clients come in behind? Not sure, would need to check.
