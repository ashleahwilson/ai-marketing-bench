For a LinkedIn post from me (Priya Nair, co-founder, Ferncliff).

Most dental offices lose a fortune to no-shows and just accept it.

What we saw: our pilot clinic switched its reminders from one-way texts to two-way texts (the patient replies C to confirm or R to reschedule). Over 3 months their no-show rate went from 14% to 9%.

Why I think it worked: rescheduling is one tap, so people who would have skipped the appointment rebook instead.
