# Call notes: Hannah and Dev, content planning, Oct 20

Hannah's three things she keeps telling owners:

1. Your POS deposit is not your sales. Card fees, tips and refunds come out first, so match deposits to sales reports, not to bank totals. She sees owners overstate sales every month because of this.

2. Sales tax filings are included on every plan. Owners still pay a second person to do them. Hannah: "You're paying twice for the same filing."

3. Count inventory on the last night of the month. Without a count, food cost is a guess. One client counted for the first time and found their food cost was 6 points higher than they thought.

Also: a prospect asked Hannah if we're AI-powered. She said no: reconciliation from Toast, Square and Lightspeed is automatic, but a person closes your books.
