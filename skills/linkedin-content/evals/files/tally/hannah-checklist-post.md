I've done month-end for restaurants for nine years. The checklist I give every new client has five things on it: bank and card reconciliations, POS deposits matched to sales, invoices entered by the 2nd, payroll accrued, and inventory counted on the last night of the month.

The one owners skip is inventory. Without a count, your food cost is a guess.

I've turned the checklist into a one-page PDF.
