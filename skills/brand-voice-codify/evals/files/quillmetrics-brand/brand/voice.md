# Voice

Last reviewed: 2026-10-08

## Principles

- Confident and concrete. Say what the product does and what it costs [source: marketing lead, 2026-10-08 · confirmed]
- US spelling throughout (analyze, color, optimize) [source: marketing lead, 2026-10-08 · confirmed]

## Words we use

- revenue, dashboard, merchants, Seamless Sync (always capitalized, it's a product name) [source: marketing lead, 2026-10-08 · confirmed]

## Words we never use

- leverage, synergy, actionable insights, data-driven [source: marketing lead, 2026-10-08 · confirmed]

## By channel

### LinkedIn
- A little playful is fine. One exclamation mark per post is fine. No hashtags [source: marketing lead, 2026-10-08 · confirmed]

### Email
- No exclamation marks. Under 150 words [source: marketing lead, 2026-10-08 · confirmed]
