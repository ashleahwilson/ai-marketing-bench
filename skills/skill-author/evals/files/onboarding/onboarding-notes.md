## What we drafted and what I changed

Email 1 (day 0). Draft asked them to connect Shopify, connect Stripe, invite their team and book a call.
My correction: one action per email. Day 0 is connect your Shopify store, nothing else. The rest
goes in later emails.

Email 1 subject. Draft: "Welcome to Quillmetrics, here's everything you need to get started!"
My correction: subject lines under 45 characters, no exclamation marks. "Connect your store in 2 minutes" was approved.

Email 2 (day 3). Draft went to everyone.
My correction: day 3 only goes to people who haven't connected their store yet. If they've connected,
skip to email 3.

Email 2 body. Draft said "see your revenue in real time".
My correction: never "real time", our data refreshes every 15 minutes (that's in brand rules).

Email 3 (day 7). Draft quoted Jordan: "Quillmetrics paid for itself in a week."
My correction: no customer quotes in onboarding. They've already bought. Show them one report
they haven't opened yet instead.

All emails: sign off from a named person on the success team, first name only.

## Approved sequence (final)

1. Day 0, "Connect your store in 2 minutes": one button, connect Shopify.
2. Day 3, only if not connected, "Your dashboard is waiting": one button, connect Shopify.
3. Day 7, "The report most merchants miss": one report (refund rate by product), one button to open it.
