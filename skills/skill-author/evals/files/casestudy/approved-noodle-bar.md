# Prime cost before the month is over

"We finally know our prime cost before the month is over."

That's the owner of a three-location noodle bar in Toronto, describing the change after moving her books to Tallyhouse.

## The problem

Before, the month's numbers arrived weeks after the month ended. By the time she saw food cost and labour, the decisions that drove them were long past.

## What changed

Her bookkeeper reconciles daily sales from the POS automatically and closes the month with a P&L. Prime cost, food cost and labour % sit on one dashboard.

## The result

She sees prime cost while the month is still running, and adjusts orders and schedules before the month closes, not after.
