# Promo writer

Writes a promotional email from a short brief.

## Steps

1. Read the brief and `brand/voice.md`.
2. Write three subject lines under 45 characters and one preview line.
3. Write the body: the offer in the first sentence, one reason to act now, one button.
4. Check the offer terms against `brand/offer.md`. Never invent a discount or deadline.

See [references/tone.md](references/tone.md) for tone and [references/deep/offers.md](references/deep/offers.md) for offer formats.

An example is in [assets/examples/example.md](assets/examples/example.md).
