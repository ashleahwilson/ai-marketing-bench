**Recommendation:** Send 4 emails: three service emails that get the statements in and confirm the work, then one invitation to the monthly plan 3 days after the catch-up is delivered.

**The sequence**

| # | Job | Timing | Subject | CTA | Exit or branch |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm the purchase, ask for statements | Immediately | Your catch-up: what we need from you | Upload your statements | All months uploaded: skip to 3 |
| 2 | Remind about missing statements | Day 3, again day 7 | Still missing a few statements | Upload the missing months | All months uploaded: go to 3 |
| 3 | Confirm everything is in | Same day as last upload | We have everything we need | Reply with questions | Catch-up delivered: go to 4 |
| 4 | Invite them onto the monthly plan | 3 days after delivery | Stay caught up from here | Reply "monthly" | Replies, unsubscribes, or ends |

**Emails:** in email/sequence-catch-up.md.

**Consent and compliance:** these are paying clients. Emails 1 to 3 are about the work they bought, so they carry your address but no unsubscribe. Email 4 promotes the monthly plan, so it has the full footer: your Toronto address and the unsubscribe link. Anyone already on a monthly plan never enters.

**Check before sending:**
1. How long catch-up takes once statements are in. Email 3 has a marked gap for it, because brand memory doesn't say and a promised date would be a guess.
2. A link to start the monthly plan, if you have one. Until then, email 4 asks for a reply.
