# Voice

Last reviewed: 2026-10-10

## Principles

- Plain and practical; short sentences [source: team review with Hannah Ruiz, 2026-10-10 · confirmed]
- On the owner's side: their time belongs "in the kitchen, not in spreadsheets" [source: website /about, 2026-10-10 · confirmed]
- Canadian spelling: labour, colour, cheque, centre [source: team review with Hannah Ruiz, 2026-10-10 · confirmed]

## Do

- Use concrete time and money terms (days to close, prime cost, food cost %) [source: team review, 2026-10-10 · confirmed]
- Talk about "your bookkeeper", a person, not "the platform" or "the software" [source: team review, 2026-10-10 · confirmed]

## Don't

- Don't position it as a chatbot or as automation alone. The human bookkeeper is the point [source: website /home, 2026-10-10 · confirmed]
- No exclamation marks in email [source: team review, 2026-10-10 · confirmed]

## Words we use

- prime cost, P&L, food cost, labour %, month-end, close, your bookkeeper [source: website /home, 2026-10-10 · confirmed]

## Words we never use

- AI-powered, revolutionary, revolutionize, solution, seamless, game-changer, hassle-free [source: team review with Hannah Ruiz, 2026-10-10 · confirmed]

## By channel

### LinkedIn
- Written in the first person by a founder (Dev or Hannah). Under 150 words. At most one exclamation mark and two hashtags [source: team review, 2026-10-10 · confirmed]

### Email
- Under 120 words. No exclamation marks. Sign off with a first name only [source: team review, 2026-10-10 · confirmed]

### Website
- Second person ("you"). One idea per section [source: team review, 2026-10-10 · confirmed]

## Examples

- On voice: "Your bookkeeper has run restaurant books before. You'll know your prime cost before month-end." [source: team review, 2026-10-10 · confirmed]
