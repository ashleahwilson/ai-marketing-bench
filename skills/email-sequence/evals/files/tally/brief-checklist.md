# Email brief: Prime Cost Checklist nurture

- Sequence type: nurture after a lead magnet
- Lead magnet: The Prime Cost Checklist (a two-page PDF), https://tallyhouse.example/checklist.pdf
- Entry trigger: submits the form at https://tallyhouse.example/checklist
- Consent: the form has an unticked checkbox: "Send me the checklist and occasional emails from Tallyhouse. Unsubscribe anytime." Only people who tick it enter this sequence. Each signup is stored with a timestamp.
- Audience: single-location restaurant owners in Canada and the US
- Goal: book a free month-end review, https://tallyhouse.example/review
- Length: 4 to 5 emails over about two weeks
- Sender: Hannah Ruiz
- Must include: our speed. Say we close your books in 3 days, not 3 weeks.
- Exit: books a review, or unsubscribes. After the last email, people who didn't book move to the monthly newsletter.
