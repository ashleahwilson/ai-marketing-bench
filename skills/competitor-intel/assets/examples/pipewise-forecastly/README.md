# Worked example: Pipewise vs Forecastly

Pipewise (pipeline forecasting software) and Forecastly are fictional. No results are claimed.

## The request

> Build a battlecard on Forecastly. Their pricing page, two reviews and our deal notes are in intel/. Brand memory is in brand/.

## Input

- `brand/`: offer, proof (one named result), claims (one approved, one needing substantiation), rules
- `intel/forecastly-pricing.txt`: their pricing page, saved 2026-10-02
- `intel/forecastly-reviews.md`: two reviews, copied 2026-10-03
- `intel/winloss-notes.md`: four deals, exported 2026-10-04

## Output

- `competitors/forecastly-battlecard.md`: the battlecard
- `brand/competitors.md`: the new Forecastly section, in brand-memory format

## The reply

**Recommendation:** Compete hard for HubSpot teams and anyone who needs a forecast live within a month. When the buyer wants call recording in the same tool, say plainly that you don't record calls and qualify early.

**Battlecard:** competitors/forecastly-battlecard.md · **Profile:** brand/competitors.md (new Forecastly section)

**Where they're strong:** call recording and transcription in the same tool; both lost deals went to them on it.
**Where you win:** 30-day onboarding with a named analyst (approved claim); Corvid Analytics, within 4% of actual for three quarters; HubSpot support.
**When you lose:** both losses were Salesforce teams of 150+ reps that wanted call recording. Four deals is too few to call it a pattern.

**Left out:**
- "The most accurate forecast in B2B": restricted until it's substantiated. The Corvid result is the defensible version.
- "Forecastly can't connect to HubSpot": their page doesn't say so. One review and one deal describe CSV uploads, so it's a question for the buyer, not a claim.

**Needed from you:**
1. Enterprise pricing, if a prospect has a quote
2. More deal notes against Forecastly

## What to notice

- Prices keep their conditions ("billed annually, minimum 10 users"); Enterprise is "not shown", not guessed.
- Forecastly's "up to 95%" accuracy is recorded as their claim.
- Each landmine traces to a review, with the review's own date beside the capture date.
- The objection on call recording concedes the point instead of dodging it.
