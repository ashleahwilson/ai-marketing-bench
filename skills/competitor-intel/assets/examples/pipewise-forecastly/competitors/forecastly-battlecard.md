# Battlecard: Forecastly (for Pipewise)

Status: draft · Built: 2026-10-06
Oldest capture: 2026-10-02 · Refresh by: 2026-12-31

## Recommendation
Compete hard for HubSpot teams and teams that want to be live within a month. Where the buyer wants call recording in the same tool, say plainly we don't record calls and qualify early.

## Who they are
- Forecasting and call intelligence for revenue teams [source: forecastly-pricing.txt (saved page), 2026-10-02 · confirmed]
- Lists a Salesforce integration; no other CRM is named on the page [source: forecastly-pricing.txt, 2026-10-02 · confirmed]

| Enterprise | not shown ("contact us") | not shown | forecastly-pricing.txt, 2026-10-02 |

## Where they're strong
- Call recording and transcription in the same tool as the forecast; one reviewer called it "a big win" (review dated 2026-08-14) [source: forecastly-pricing.txt, 2026-10-02; forecastly-reviews.md, 2026-10-03 · confirmed]
- Both lost deals went to them on call recording [source: winloss-notes.md, 2026-10-04 · confirmed]

## Where we win
- Live in a month: 30-day onboarding with a named analyst · proof: approved claim, named analyst for the first 30 days [source: brand/claims.md, 2026-10-05]
- Forecast accuracy we can show · proof: Corvid Analytics (85 reps), within 4% of actual for three straight quarters [source: brand/proof.md, 2026-08]
- HubSpot teams · proof: we work with Salesforce and HubSpot [source: brand/offer.md, 2026-10-05]

## When we lose to them and why
- 4 deals, 2026-07 to 2026-09: 2 won, 2 lost [source: winloss-notes.md, 2026-10-04 · confirmed]
- Both losses were Salesforce teams of 150+ reps that wanted call recording, or already had it [source: winloss-notes.md, 2026-10-04 · confirmed]
- Sample: 4 deals, too few to call it a pattern; watch the next quarter [source: winloss-notes.md, 2026-10-04 · confirmed]

## Landmines
1. "How does your CRM data get into the forecast today, and how often?" · exposes: one reviewer on HubSpot (review dated 2026-06-02) uploads a CSV weekly [source: forecastly-reviews.md, 2026-10-03 · provisional]
2. "How long can you wait before the forecast is live?" · exposes: one reviewer's admin took about two months to set up (review dated 2026-08-14) [source: forecastly-reviews.md, 2026-10-03 · provisional]

## Objections
| They say | We say | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| "Forecastly also records our calls." | True, and we don't. If call recording in one tool is a must, they're a fair choice. If the forecast is the problem, we're live in 30 days with a named analyst. | brand/claims.md (named analyst); brand/offer.md (We don't do) |
| "Forecastly says 95% accuracy." | That's their figure, and we haven't seen how it's measured. Here's ours, with a named customer. | Corvid Analytics, within 4% for three quarters (brand/proof.md) |
