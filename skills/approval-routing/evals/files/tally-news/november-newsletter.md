# Tallyhouse notes: November

Version: v1

Hi there,

November is when the holiday rush starts to show up in your numbers. Three things to watch this month:

1. **Prime cost.** Holiday menus often carry higher food cost. Check it weekly, not monthly.
2. **Labour.** Extra shifts add up fast. Compare scheduled hours with sales every Monday.
3. **Gift cards.** Gift card sales aren't revenue until they're redeemed. Your bookkeeper records them as a liability.
