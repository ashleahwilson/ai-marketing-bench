# Reading your restaurant P&L in ten minutes

Version: v1
Author: Hannah Ruiz

Your P&L tells you three things that matter every month: what you sold, what it cost you to sell it, and what was left.

## Start with prime cost

Add food cost and labour. That's your prime cost. Look at it as a percentage of sales, and watch which way it moves month to month.

## Then look at the lines that surprised you

A jump in a single line usually has a single cause: a supplier price change, a new hire, a broken cooler.
