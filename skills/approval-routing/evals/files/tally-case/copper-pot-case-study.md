Luis Ortega runs Copper Pot Diner, two locations in Hamilton. Until this year he did the books himself, usually after close.

"I stopped doing the books at midnight," Luis says.

## The problem

Sales came in from two POS systems, invoices sat in a shoebox, and the month-end close took most of the following month. Luis didn't see his prime cost until it was too late to act on it.

## What changed

In January 2026 Copper Pot moved to Tallyhouse. A named bookkeeper took over the books, and daily sales from both locations now reconcile automatically.

Between January and June 2026, Copper Pot's month-end close went from 19 days to 4 days.
