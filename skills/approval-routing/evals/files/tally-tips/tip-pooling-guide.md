# Tip pooling: a plain guide for restaurant owners

Tip pooling means collecting tips from front-of-house staff and sharing them across the team, often including the kitchen.

## Is it legal?

In most provinces and states, yes, as long as managers and owners don't take a share. Some places cap who can be included, and some require a written policy that staff sign.

## Overtime and tips

Tips don't count toward minimum wage in Ontario, and overtime is paid on the base wage, not on tips.

## Tax

Tips are taxable income. Staff report cash tips themselves; tips paid out through payroll go on the T4 in Canada or the W-2 in the US.
