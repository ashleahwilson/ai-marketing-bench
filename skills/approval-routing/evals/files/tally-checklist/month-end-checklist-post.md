# A five-step month-end checklist for restaurant owners

Version: v1

Month-end goes faster when nothing is waiting for you on the last day. Here's the short list.

1. **Reconcile sales.** Make sure your POS totals match your bank deposits. Tallyhouse reconciles Toast, Square and Lightspeed sales automatically every day.
2. **Count inventory.** Count the walk-in and dry storage on the same day every month.
3. **Collect invoices.** Get every supplier invoice in before you close.
4. **Review labour.** Check scheduled hours against what was paid.
5. **Read your P&L.** Start with prime cost: food cost plus labour, as a share of sales.

Questions about any of these? Your bookkeeper can walk you through them.
