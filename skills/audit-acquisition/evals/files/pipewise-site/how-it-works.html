How it works

Connect

Connect Salesforce or HubSpot in about ten minutes. Pipewise reads deal stages, activity and close dates; it never writes to your CRM.

Forecast

Pipewise builds your weekly sales forecast from deal activity in Salesforce or HubSpot and flags every deal at risk, so the forecast call starts from data instead of gut feel.

Review

Each Monday, sales managers get the forecast, the deals that slipped, and the deals with no activity in 14 days.
