Dental scheduling that fills cancelled appointments

Ferncliff is scheduling software for dental clinics with 1 to 10 chairs. When a patient cancels, it offers the slot to your waitlist by text, and it sends reminders patients can confirm with one reply.

Book a demo

What it does

  • Waitlist fill for cancelled slots
  • Two-way text reminders
  • Online booking on your website

Who it's for

Independent dental clinics and small groups. Not built for hospital dental departments.
