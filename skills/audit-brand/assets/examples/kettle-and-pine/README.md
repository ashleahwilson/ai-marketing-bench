# Worked example: Kettle & Pine Catering

A fictional Vancouver catering company audits its own brand. The user asked:

> Brand audit for Kettle & Pine. Our site is in site/, the October newsletter is in channels/, and our brand memory is in brand/.

- Inputs: `site/` (three saved pages), `channels/newsletter-october.md`, `brand/` (BRAND.md, voice.md, rules.md, proof.md).
- Outputs: `audits/kettleandpine.example/brand.md` (the report) and `dim-brand.json` (the B1–B5 checklist in the marketing audit's format).

What it shows: a brand that is mostly consistent, where one sentence on one page breaks a rule, two never-use words and the spelling convention at once; an unsupported figure beside credible proof; and the email channel rule checked and met. The reply to the user would lead with the verdict, the score of 57, the three findings and the 48-hour actions.
