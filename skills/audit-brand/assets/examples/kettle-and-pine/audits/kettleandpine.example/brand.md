# Brand audit: Kettle & Pine Catering
2026-10-24 · Founder-led (team of 11, owners write the newsletter, no marketing roles) · Pages and pieces reviewed: 4 · Brand memory: the company's · Confidence: Medium

## Verdict
Keep the voice you have on the home page and in the newsletter; rewrite the one menus line that breaks it, and source or cut "500+ events".
Brand score: 57/100 (B1–B5 below).

## The three things that matter most
1. **The menus page breaks your own rules in one sentence.** Evidence, menus.html: "Our curated seasonal menus elevate every gathering with farm-to-table flavor." Your rules say never "farm-to-table" ("We buy from farms, but we're not a farm"), "curated" and "elevate" are on your never-use list, and "flavor" is US spelling where you write Canadian. Fix: replace it with a line in your own voice, for example "Our menus change with what the Fraser Valley farms send us."
2. **"500+ events" has no source.** Evidence, index.html: "Trusted for 500+ events across the Lower Mainland." It isn't in your brand memory. Fix: count events from your booking records and add the figure to brand memory with its source, or cut the line. The Northshore quote beside it already does the work.
3. **Small care gaps on the menus page.** Evidence, menus.html: the autumn menu photo has no alt text, and the heading reads "harvest table" where every other page writes "Harvest Table". Fix: add alt text that describes the photo, and capitalise the heading.

## Naming and terminology
- "Harvest Table" on index.html and in the newsletter; "harvest table" as a heading on menus.html. Use "Harvest Table", the form your brand memory records.
- "Kettle & Pine" is written the same way in every title, footer and sign-off.

## Proof and trust
- Credible: Dana Whitfield, office manager, Northshore Credit Union (index.html), quoted exactly as in your proof file, permission yes. Founders named with their experience (about.html: "Ines Moreau has cooked in Vancouver kitchens for 12 years").
- Unsupported: "Trusted for 500+ events across the Lower Mainland" (index.html). See finding 2.
- Trust signals: street address, email, phone and a privacy link in every page footer.
- Missing: no reviews or ratings on the site. That's not a gap to fill with a number; if you have a review profile, link it.
