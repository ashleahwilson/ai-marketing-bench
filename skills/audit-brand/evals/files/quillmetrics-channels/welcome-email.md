Quillmetrics brings your Shopify and Stripe data into one revenue dashboard. Over the next few days, we'll walk you through everything you need to get started, from connecting your store to reading your first report.

Here's what to do first. Connect Shopify in Settings > Integrations. Then connect Stripe on the same screen. Once both are connected, turn on Seamless Sync and your dashboard will refresh every 15 minutes from then on.

When your data has loaded, open the Revenue tab. You'll see gross sales, refunds, Stripe fees and payouts side by side for the last 90 days. On a large store, the first load can take a few minutes, so it's worth starting it before lunch. Most merchants start by checking last month's payouts against what their bank received.
