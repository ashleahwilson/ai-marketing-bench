# Tallyhouse: marketing health

**Score: 45/100, grade D.** The foundations need rebuilding before more spend on growth. The weakest areas are acquisition and conversion.

## Three findings
1. **The "3 days" headline has no proof on the site.** Quote one client's measured close time, or change the headline to a claim you can show. (index.html)
2. **The contact form asks for 10 fields, including revenue and phone.** Cut it to four: first name, email, restaurant, locations. Ask the rest on the call. (contact.html)
3. **Form submissions are not counted as conversions.** Track a lead event on successful submit, so you can see what the site produces. (all pages)

## The first 48 hours
- Remove the "AI-powered" banner above the "not a chatbot" line on the home page.
- Shorten the contact form to four fields.
- Add the analytics setting to pricing and contact, and track form submissions.

## How this was done
A read of the four saved pages (home, pricing, about, contact), scored against a fixed set of checks. Traffic, ad spend and conversion rates were not visible and are not included.
