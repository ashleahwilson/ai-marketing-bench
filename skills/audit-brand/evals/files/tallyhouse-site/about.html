About TallyHouse

Tallyhouse Inc. is committed to delivering best-in-class financial solutions that empower hospitality stakeholders to achieve superior operational outcomes across their organizations.

The company was founded in 2021 by Dev Patel, a former restaurant controller, and Hannah Ruiz, a CPA. The organization comprises 14 professionals based in Toronto and serves clients across Canada and the United States.

Every bookkeeper on the team has worked in hospitality finance. Tallyhouse does not serve retail, e-commerce or franchises with more than 15 locations.
