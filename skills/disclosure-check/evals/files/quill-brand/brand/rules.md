## Disclosures

- Affiliate links: put this sentence before the first affiliate link, word for word: "We earn a commission if you buy through some links in this post. It doesn't change what we recommend." [source: legal review, 2026-09-15 · confirmed]
- Sponsored newsletter sections: the section heading starts with "Sponsored" [source: legal review, 2026-09-15 · confirmed]
