# 5 inventory apps worth paying for in 2026

If your stock counts and your revenue numbers disagree, your reports are only half right. Here are five inventory apps we'd look at first, and what each one does best.

## 1. Stockline

Best for stores selling from more than one warehouse. Counts sync across locations and sales channels. [Try Stockline](https://stockline.example/?ref=quillmetrics)

## 2. Packwell

Best for stores that bundle products. Bundles draw down each component's stock automatically. [See Packwell's plans](https://packwell.example/pricing?aff=quill22)

## 3. Reorderly

Best for reordering. It suggests purchase orders from your sales pace. [Visit Reorderly](https://reorderly.example)

## 4. Binsight

Best for small warehouses that still count by hand. Scan bins with a phone. [Get Binsight](https://quillmetrics.example/go/binsight)

## 5. Countwise

Best for stores on a tight budget. The free plan covers one location. [See Countwise features](https://countwise.example/features)
