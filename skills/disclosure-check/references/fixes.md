# Fix text and placement

Every finding ends with text the user can paste and the exact place it goes. Start from the brand's own wording in `brand/rules.md`; use these only where the brand has none. Fill the brackets only from the piece or brand memory. If a fact isn't there, leave the bracket in and list it under **Needs your input**.

## Creator and sponsored posts

| Situation | Text | Place |
|---|---|---|
| Paid post | `Ad · Paid partnership with @[brand]` | First line of the caption, before any other text |
| Gifted product only | `Gifted by @[brand]` | First line |
| Creator earns commission on a code or link | `Ad · I earn a commission on sign-ups with my code` | First line (or with the code, if the code is in the first line) |
| Video or story | Say "this is a paid partnership with [brand]" in the first seconds, and show "Ad" on screen | Start of the video, plus the caption |

Plus: turn on the platform's paid-partnership label, as well as the words. Remove the buried hashtag only after the first-line disclosure is in (leaving it is harmless).

## Testimonials with a connection

Put the connection in the attribution line, in plain words:
- Employee: `— [Name], [role] at [Brand]`, or under the quote: `[Name] works at [Brand] as a [role].`
- Incentive: `[Name] received [what they received, from brand memory] for sharing their experience.`
- Investor, founder, family: `[Name] is an investor in [Brand].`

If the current attribution misleads (a former business presented as current, an employee presented as a customer), replace it rather than adding a second line that contradicts it.

## Affiliate links

`[Brand's house sentence, word for word]`, or if none: `We earn a commission if you buy through some links in this post. It doesn't change what we recommend.` Place it in the intro, before the first affiliate link. List which links are affiliate links (tracking parameters such as `?ref=`, `?aff=`, `/go/`) and which aren't.

## Prices, fees and trials

- "From" price: `From $[x]/user/month, billed annually (minimum [n] users)` in the price itself, not a footnote.
- Mandatory fee: `Plus a one-time $[x] onboarding fee` directly under the price, or include it in the price.
- Trial, next to the sign-up button: `Free for [n] days, then $[price]/[period] unless you cancel before [when]. Cancel anytime in [where].` Every bracket must come from the page or the user. A trial plan with no price on the page keeps `[price]` blank and goes under **Needs your input**.
- Remove "no strings", "no commitment" or "risk-free" when a card is required or billing starts automatically.

## Contests and giveaways

In the post (short):
`No purchase necessary. Open to [eligibility] [age]+. Closes [date, time, time zone]. Winner must answer a skill-testing question (Canada). Odds depend on the number of entries. Full rules: [link]. Not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Instagram.`

Full rules page must cover: sponsor name and address, eligibility and exclusions, entry period, how to enter (including a free method if any purchase is involved), prize description and approximate retail value, number of prizes, odds, how and when the winner is chosen and contacted, the skill-testing question, the platform release, and privacy. Prize value comes from brand memory (show the basis, for example 12 × $349) or the user. Then: "Have a lawyer review the full rules before it goes live."

## Email

Footer:
```
[Legal name], [mailing address from brand/senders.md]
[Unsubscribe link from your email platform] · or the sender's opt-out line from brand/senders.md
```
Subject: replace a fake "Re:" or "Fwd:" with a plain subject drawn from the email's own words.

## AI imagery

Use the brand's label word for word (for example `AI-generated image`), on the image or directly under it. If a generated person is presented as a customer, or quoted, remove the quote and the "customer" framing: a disclosure doesn't fix it.

## When the client wants the disclosure gone

Keep it. One or two sentences on why: readers have to know it's paid before they act, and the rule (name it) puts the responsibility on the brand. Then make it lighter, never vaguer or later:
- Shorter: `Sponsored` or `Ad` instead of a full sentence. Drop a second, redundant disclosure line.
- Smaller: a small label in the section heading or above the image instead of a banner, as long as it's still readable before the link.
- Native: the platform's paid-partnership label plus a short "Ad" in the first line.

Never offer: moving it to the footer, after "more", into hashtags, behind a link or hover, or replacing it with "partner", "friends at", "collab" or "thanks to". Give the lighter version as paste-ready text, so they can still send on time.
