## For the writer

### Angle
Owners who are behind feel judged and assume the fix is a big, expensive clean-up. Take the shame out of it: show that catching up is a sequence of single months, quoted per month, and that the review tells them how many months there are before they commit to anything.

### Outline
1. **You're not the only one behind.** Name the situation in the owner's words (books weeks late, nights in spreadsheets). No statistics on how many owners are behind.
2. **What catching up actually involves.** One month at a time: reconcile sales, record costs, close the month. Daily sales come in automatically from Toast, Square or Lightspeed, which shortens the work.
3. **How it's priced.** Catch-up is quoted per month of backlog, so the owner knows the cost before work starts. Don't state a price: none is recorded.
4. **What you get once you're caught up.** A monthly P&L, sales tax filings and a named bookkeeper on every plan; single location is $349/month.
5. **Start with the review.** The free month-end review shows how far behind the books are. CTA.
