# Worked example: a coffee roaster's product page

Kettle & Crumb is a fictional coffee roaster in Halifax that sells online in Canada and the US. The files:
- `product-page.md`: the page as drafted
- `brand/`: the brand memory the review reads
- `claims/product-page-claims-review.md`: the review, which is also the reply

## Why Hold for legal review

Three claims are high risk, so the page as written needs a lawyer before it runs: an acid comparison with a stomach-health effect and no test, a "fair trade" claim the owners have banned because the beans aren't certified, and a compostable-pod claim when nobody has confirmed what the pods are made of. The rewrites drop all three, so the fast path is to use them and publish.

## What it didn't flag

- **"The smoothest cup you'll ever pour."** Taste opinion no one could measure. Not a claim.
- **Roasted weekly in Halifax, the bean origins, the prices.** Each is confirmed in brand memory.

## What it fixed without new facts

"Cancel anytime" and "Free shipping, always" were nearly true. Each rewrite adds the condition brand memory already records (cancel before the next ship date; orders over $40), and nothing else.
