# Win/loss notes (sales team, 2025-2026)

## Won: Northgate Smiles (12 locations), 2025-07
Opening their 12th clinic and the ops lead was rebuilding three rotas a week by hand. Chose us over the PMS calendar because it handles hygienists who float between clinics.

## Won: Summit Ridge Dental (11 locations), 2026-03
New regional operations director hired in January; first project was "get the scheduling out of spreadsheets". Referred in by Maplecrest.

## Won: Kestrel Dental Studio (4 locations), 2025-10
Office manager quit and the owner found the rota lived in her personal spreadsheet. Signed within a month.

## Lost: Granite Bay Dental (13 locations), 2025-11
Two years into a ChairTime contract. Liked the product; switching cost and retraining 100 staff mid-contract was the blocker. Revisit at renewal (their contract ends 2027-03).

## Lost: Evergreen Dental Partners (42 locations), 2025-10
Six months in IT security review. They required a SOC 2 report we don't have.

## Lost: Birchwood Dental Care (4 locations), 2026-01
Decided the PMS calendar is good enough. Only one hygienist floats between sites.

## Churned: Oakview Dental (1 location), 2025-09
Owner-dentist builds the schedule herself on Sunday nights. Said she never logged in after the first month.

## Churned: Westfield Dental Group (10 locations), 2026-04
Acquired by a group that runs ChairTime everywhere; moved back after the acquisition.
