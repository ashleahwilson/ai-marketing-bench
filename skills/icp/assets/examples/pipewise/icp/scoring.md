# ICP scoring rubric: Pipewise

Version 1, written 2026-10-20. Status: evidence-based.
Basis: 26 closed records from data/deals.csv (2 open excluded); 5 win/loss notes; 1 call note. Only the software and 50–199 employee findings rest on 10+ records; the rest are hypotheses.
Max points: 100 (unknown values score nothing).
Tiers: A 70+, B 45+, C 25+

## Criteria

| Criterion | Points | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Employees 50–199 | 40 | 12 of 15 won; 1 of 12 churned |
| Employees 20–49 | 10 | 4 of 9 won in the 10–49 band, 2 of 4 churned (hypothesis) |
| Industry: software | 30 | 12 of 16 won; 2 of 12 churned |
| Industry: manufacturing, logistics | -20 | manufacturing 0 of 3 won, logistics 0 of 1 (hypothesis) |
| Signal new sales leader: yes | 30 | 3 of 3 win notes mention a new VP Sales or first sales manager (hypothesis) |

## Disqualifiers

| Criterion | Points | Reason | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sales reps under 5 | DQ | the review needs a manager to run it | brand/offer.md; Parallax CRM churned with four reps and no manager (1 win/loss note) |

## Notes

- Back-test on the 26 closed deals: 9 of the 13 retained customers score A, against 1 of the 3 churned and 1 of the 10 lost. The export has no signal or rep-count columns, so no deal got those points.
