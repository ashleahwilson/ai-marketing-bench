# Audiences

Last reviewed: 2026-10-20
Evidence base: 26 closed records (data/deals.csv; 2 open deals excluded), 5 win/loss notes, 1 call note

## Segments

- B2B software companies with 50–199 employees: 10 of 11 won, 1 of 10 churned, median deal $15,600 (n=10) [source: data/deals.csv, 2026-10-20 · confirmed]
- Fintech and healthtech companies with 50–199 employees: 1 of 1 won in each; only 2 records, too few to rank [source: data/deals.csv, 2026-10-20 · provisional]
- Companies with 10–49 employees: 4 of 9 won and 2 of those 4 churned; only 9 records, too few to be sure, so watch rather than target [source: data/deals.csv, 2026-10-20 · provisional]
- Canada and the US: 6 of 10 won in Canada, 10 of 16 in the US; no meaningful difference [source: data/deals.csv, 2026-10-20 · confirmed]

## Triggers and signals

- A new VP Sales or first sales manager: mentioned in 3 of 3 win notes [source: data/win-loss.md, 2026-10-20 · provisional]

## Not for

- Teams under 5 reps: the review needs a manager to run it [source: brand/offer.md, 2026-09-15 · confirmed]
- Manufacturing and logistics: 0 of 3 and 0 of 1 won; only 4 records, too few to be sure, so revisit with more deals [source: data/deals.csv, 2026-10-20 · provisional]

## Personas

- Objections: "My reps won't update another tool." [source: data/calls.md, Nadia Osei, 2026-05-12 · confirmed]
- Their words: "I walk into the forecast call knowing which three deals are actually at risk." [source: data/calls.md, Nadia Osei, 2026-05-12 · confirmed]
