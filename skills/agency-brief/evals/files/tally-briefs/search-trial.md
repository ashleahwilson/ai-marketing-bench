## 1. Background

Tallyhouse is a restaurant bookkeeping firm based in Toronto. We do monthly bookkeeping for independent restaurants and small groups of 1–15 locations in Canada and the US, with a monthly P&L, sales tax filings and a named bookkeeper on every plan. Daily sales reconcile automatically from Toast, Square and Lightspeed. Our bookkeepers have all worked in hospitality finance. We have never run paid search, and our site gets little organic search traffic.

## 2. Business objective

More booked free month-end reviews from single-location restaurant owners, from search, at a cost we can measure per booked review.

## 3. Audience

Owner-operators of independent restaurants, single location first, in Canada and the US. Our working view: they're measured on prime cost and cash at month end, and their books close weeks late. In their words: "know our prime cost before the month is over."

Not for: retail, e-commerce, franchises over 15 locations.

## 5. Mandatories

### Brand voice and rules
Plain and practical, short sentences, on the owner's side. Canadian spelling (labour, colour, cheque, centre). Talk about "your bookkeeper", a person, not "the platform" or "the software".

### Approved claims
No performance claims are approved yet. Product facts in the brand pack may be used, including prices: single location $349/month; groups of 2–15 locations from $299/location/month.

### Claims not to use
- "Close your books in 3 days, not 3 weeks": not approved for use.
- "P&L ready by the 3rd of every month": not approved for use.

### Legal and compliance
Ads must be truthful and every claim substantiated. Reviews and testimonials are never fabricated, bought or incentivized. The Lantern Noodle Bar quote may only be used after Dev confirms permission to name the customer.

### Accessibility
Landing page edits meet WCAG 2.2 AA.

### Ownership and access
All accounts (Google Ads, Google Analytics, Search Console, Google Business Profile) stay in Tallyhouse's name, with Tallyhouse as owner. We grant your team user access; we never share passwords. All data, campaigns, ads and reports are ours, during and after the engagement.

## 6. What not to do
- Never describe Tallyhouse as "AI-powered".
- No "revolutionary", "seamless", "game-changer", "hassle-free" or "solution".
- No exclamation marks in ads.
- No guaranteed rankings or results.
