# Tallyhouse brand pack

For the photographer on our team photo shoot. Please don't share beyond the people working on this project.

## What we sell

Monthly bookkeeping for restaurants, with a P&L every month, sales tax filings and a named bookkeeper on every plan. Daily sales reconcile automatically from Toast, Square and Lightspeed. Food cost and labour % sit on one dashboard.

## Who it's for (and who it isn't)

- Independent restaurants and small groups of 1–15 locations, in Canada and the US.
- Not retail, e-commerce, or franchises over 15 locations.
- Our working view of the owner-operator: measured on prime cost and cash at month end; tired of books closing weeks late and nights spent in spreadsheets. In their words: "know our prime cost before the month is over."

## Why customers choose us

Every one of our bookkeepers has worked in hospitality finance. The person is the point: "a real bookkeeper who knows restaurants."

## How we sound, and what that means for the pictures

- Plain and practical, on the owner's side: their time belongs "in the kitchen, not in spreadsheets."
- We talk about "your bookkeeper", a person, not "the platform" or "the software". Photos should show people working, not screens on their own.
- We don't position Tallyhouse as a chatbot or as automation alone. No robot, AI or futuristic imagery.

## Words we never use

AI-powered, revolutionary, revolutionize, solution, seamless, game-changer, hassle-free. This matters for captions and file names too.

## Claims not to make

These appear in some of our material but aren't approved for use. Please don't use them in captions or behind-the-scenes posts:
- "Close your books in 3 days, not 3 weeks"
- "P&L ready by the 3rd of every month"
