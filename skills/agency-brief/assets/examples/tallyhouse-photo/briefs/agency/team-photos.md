Status: draft (gaps marked TBD)

## 1. Background

Tallyhouse is a restaurant bookkeeping firm in Toronto, founded in 2021, with a team of 14. We do monthly bookkeeping for independent restaurants and small groups in Canada and the US. Every one of our bookkeepers has worked in hospitality finance. We're rebuilding our website and want real photos of the people who do the work.

## 2. Business objective

Show prospective clients the real bookkeepers they'd work with, so the website makes the case that a person who knows restaurants does their books.

## 3. Audience

Owner-operators of independent restaurants and small groups (1–15 locations) in Canada and the US. Our working view: they're measured on prime cost and cash at month end, and they're tired of books closing weeks late.

## 5. Mandatories

### Brand voice and rules
Plain, practical, on the owner's side. Show people working, not screens on their own. No robot, AI or futuristic imagery. Canadian spelling in any captions (labour, colour, centre).

### Approved claims
No performance claims are approved yet. Product facts in the brand pack may be used.

### Claims not to use
- "Close your books in 3 days, not 3 weeks": not approved for use.
- "P&L ready by the 3rd of every month": not approved for use.

### Legal and compliance
Signed model releases from everyone identifiable in a photo, and a location release from any restaurant. We'll confirm with Lantern Noodle Bar before you shoot there or show it.

### Accessibility
For each final image, a one-line description of what it shows, so we can write alt text.

### Ownership and access
Tallyhouse receives the final images and the unedited selects, with the right to use them on any channel without time limit. You may show up to five images in your portfolio once our site is live.

## 6. What not to do
- No "AI-powered", "revolutionary", "seamless" or "solution" in captions, file names or social posts.
- Don't photograph screens showing client financials.
- Don't post behind-the-scenes images before our site launches.
