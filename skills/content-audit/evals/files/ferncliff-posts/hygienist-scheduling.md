# Scheduling hygienists without double-booking

Hygiene schedules break down when the same room, the same hygienist or the same patient ends up in two places at once.

## Schedule by room and by person

Give every appointment both a room and a provider. A conflict on either should block the booking.

## Leave room for the dentist's exam

Recall appointments usually need a few minutes with the dentist. Build that into the template so the dentist isn't double-booked across rooms.

## Review tomorrow's schedule today

Ten minutes at the end of the day catches most conflicts before patients arrive.
