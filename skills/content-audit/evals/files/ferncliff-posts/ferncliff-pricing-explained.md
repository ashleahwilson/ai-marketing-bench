# Ferncliff pricing, explained

Ferncliff costs $119 per provider per month. There is no setup fee and no contract.

## What counts as a provider

Anyone who sees patients on the schedule: dentists and hygienists. Front-desk staff are free.

## What's included

Reminders, two-way texting, online booking and the short-notice list are included on every account.

## Billing

You're billed monthly. Add or remove providers at any time and the next invoice adjusts.
