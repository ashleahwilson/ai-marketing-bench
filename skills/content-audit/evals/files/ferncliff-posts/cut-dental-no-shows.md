# 7 ways to cut dental no-shows

No-shows cost your practice time and money. These seven steps help.

1. Send a reminder two days before the appointment.
2. Ask patients to confirm by text.
3. Let patients reschedule without calling.
4. Keep a list of patients who want earlier appointments.
5. Track no-shows by provider.
6. Remind patients the day before their appointment.
7. Follow up with patients who missed an appointment the same day.

Ferncliff can automate steps 1, 2, 3 and 6.
