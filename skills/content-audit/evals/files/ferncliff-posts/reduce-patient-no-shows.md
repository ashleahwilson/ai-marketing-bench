# How to reduce patient no-shows

Every empty chair is time your hygienist is paid for and nobody is treated. Here is what works for the practices we work with.

## Confirm twice

Send a confirmation when the appointment is booked and a reminder two days before. Patients who confirm are far more likely to show up, and the ones who can't make it tell you early enough to fill the slot.

## Make rescheduling easy

If changing an appointment means calling during office hours, patients who can't come simply don't come. A link to reschedule turns a no-show into a moved appointment.

## Keep a short-notice list

Ask patients who want an earlier slot to join a short-notice list. When a cancellation comes in, the front desk texts the list.

## Track your no-show rate

Count no-shows each week by provider and by appointment type. New-patient exams and Monday mornings are often worse than the rest.
