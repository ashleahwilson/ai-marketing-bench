# New for 2022: two-way text reminders

Starting today, patients can reply to Ferncliff reminders to confirm or cancel. Replies show up on the schedule with a color-coded status, so the front desk sees who has confirmed at a glance.

## Coming in 2023: online booking

Next year we'll add online booking, so new patients can book their first visit from your website.

## How to turn it on

Go to Settings > Reminders and switch on two-way replies.
