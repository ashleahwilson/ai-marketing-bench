# Instagram tips for dental practices

Instagram is a good place to show patients who you are before they walk in.

## Post your team

Introduce your hygienists and front-desk staff. Patients like to know who they'll meet.

## Show the office

A short video tour of the waiting room and treatment rooms helps nervous patients.

## Answer common questions

Short posts on what happens at a first visit or how whitening works get saved and shared.
