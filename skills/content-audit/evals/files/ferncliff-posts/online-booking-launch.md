# Online booking is here

Patients can now book appointments from your website. You choose which appointment types are bookable online and which providers patients can pick.

## Set it up in three steps

1. Choose the appointment types patients can book online.
2. Set the hours each provider is available for online booking.
3. Add the booking link to your website and your Google Business Profile.

## What patients see

Patients pick an appointment type, a provider and a time, then fill in their details. The appointment appears on your schedule straight away, marked as booked online.
