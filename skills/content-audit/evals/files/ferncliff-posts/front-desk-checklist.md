# A daily checklist for the front desk

A good front desk runs on routine. Here is a checklist to start and end each day.

## First thing

- Check overnight replies to reminders and update confirmations
- Call unconfirmed patients for today's first appointments
- Check the short-notice list against any overnight cancellations

## During the day

- Confirm tomorrow's appointments by early afternoon
- Fill cancellations from the short-notice list
- Note any patient who wants to be contacted for an earlier slot

## End of day

- Review tomorrow's schedule for room and provider conflicts
- Follow up with anyone who missed today's appointment
- Leave notes for the morning team
