# How much does restaurant bookkeeping cost?

Owners ask us this more than anything else, so here is a straight answer, including what you should expect to get for the money.

## What drives the price

Bookkeeping for a restaurant costs more than for a typical small business because of volume. A busy restaurant has daily sales from a POS, dozens of supplier invoices a week, tips, and payroll every two weeks. The more of that is reconciled by hand, the more you pay.

## What Tallyhouse costs

A single location is $299/month. Groups of 2 to 15 locations start from $299 per location per month. Every plan includes a monthly P&L, sales tax filings and a named bookkeeper. If you are behind, catch-up bookkeeping is quoted per month of backlog.

## What a general bookkeeper costs

A general bookkeeper may quote less per hour, but you will often pay for the hours they spend learning how restaurant sales, tips and food cost work. Ask any bookkeeper how they handle daily POS reconciliation before you compare prices.

## What to ask before you sign

- Is the price fixed each month, or billed by the hour?
- Are sales tax filings included?
- Will I have one named bookkeeper or a queue?
- How do you get sales data from my POS?

## Is it worth it?

The better question is what the books cost you now. Count the evenings you spend in spreadsheets and the decisions you make without current numbers.
