# Food cost percentage: the formula and a target range

Food cost percentage tells you how much of each sales dollar goes to the ingredients on the plate.

## The formula

Food cost % = (beginning inventory + purchases − ending inventory) / food sales

Use food sales only, not total sales, or your bar will hide problems in the kitchen.

## How to use it

Track it weekly with the same counting method each time. A one-week jump usually means a counting error or a big delivery. A rise over three or four weeks means something real changed: prices, portions or waste.

## Finding your target range

Your range depends on your concept and menu. A steakhouse and a noodle bar will never share a number. Set your target from your own last six months, then work on the items that move it most.

## The fastest wins

- Re-cost your top ten dishes when supplier prices change
- Check portioning on the line during a busy service
- Track waste for two weeks before changing anything else
