# How to choose a restaurant bookkeeper

Choosing a restaurant bookkeeper comes down to three things: restaurant experience, how they get your sales data, and what is included in the price.

## Restaurant experience

Ask whether they have worked in hospitality finance. Restaurant books have tips, daily sales and food cost that a general bookkeeper may not know well.

## Sales data

Ask how they get data from your POS. Daily automatic reconciliation is better than monthly exports.

## What's included

Ask whether sales tax filings and a monthly P&L are included, and whether you will have one named bookkeeper.

## Questions to ask

Ask about their price, their month-end timing and what they need from you.


## Want a second pair of eyes?

Book a free month-end review. Your bookkeeper will walk through last month's numbers with you and show you where the time and money went. It takes about thirty minutes and there is nothing to install.
