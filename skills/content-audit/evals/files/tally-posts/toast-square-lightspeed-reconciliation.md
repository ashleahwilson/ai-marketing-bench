# How POS reconciliation works with Toast, Square and Lightspeed

If you use Toast, Square or Lightspeed, your daily sales are reconciled automatically. Here is what that means in practice.

## What gets reconciled

Each day's sales, taxes, tips, discounts and card deposits are matched against your bank account. Differences are flagged to your bookkeeper, not buried until month end.

## What you need to do

Connect your POS once. After that, there is nothing to export. If you change POS systems, tell your bookkeeper before the switch so no days are missed.

## What your bookkeeper still checks

Automatic matching handles the volume. Your bookkeeper still reviews refunds, voids, cash deposits and anything that doesn't match, and asks you about it in plain language.

## Multiple locations

Each location's POS connects separately, and your bookkeeper keeps each location's books in its own class so you can compare them.

## Questions owners ask

- Do you need my POS password? No. You connect the POS from your own account.
- What about cash? Cash deposits are matched from your bank statement.


## Want a second pair of eyes?

Book a free month-end review. Your bookkeeper will walk through last month's numbers with you and show you where the time and money went. It takes about thirty minutes and there is nothing to install.
