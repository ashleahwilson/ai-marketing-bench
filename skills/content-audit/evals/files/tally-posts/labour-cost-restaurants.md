# Labour cost: what to track each week

Labour is the other half of prime cost, and it moves faster than food cost because the schedule changes every week.

## The number to watch

Labour % = total labour cost / net sales

Total labour cost includes wages, salaries, payroll taxes and benefits, not just hourly wages.

## Track it by day part

A weekly labour % can look fine while lunch is overstaffed and dinner is short. Split it by day part if your scheduling tool allows it.

## What to compare it with

Compare labour % with the same week last year and with your forecast for the week. A gap tells you whether the schedule matched the business you actually did.

## When to act

If labour % is above target for two weeks in a row, look at the schedule before you look at wages. Overlapping shifts and early starts are the usual cause.
