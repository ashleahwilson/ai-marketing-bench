# Bookkeeping tips for Shopify sellers

Selling on Shopify brings its own bookkeeping headaches: payouts that bundle fees and refunds, sales tax across states and provinces, and inventory held by a third-party warehouse.

## Separate payouts from sales

A Shopify payout is not your sales figure. Record gross sales, fees and refunds separately so your margins are real.

## Track inventory at the warehouse

If a fulfilment partner holds your stock, reconcile their counts monthly.

## Sales tax

Register where you have to, and keep a record of where each order shipped.
