# Close your books in 3 days, not 3 weeks

Most restaurant owners see last month's numbers somewhere around the middle of this month. By then it is too late to act on them.

## Close your books in 3 days, not 3 weeks

With Tallyhouse, your books close in 3 days, not 3 weeks. Daily sales come in automatically from Toast, Square and Lightspeed, so your bookkeeper is not waiting on exports at month end.

## What a fast close changes

When the P&L arrives early, you can still act on it. You can change next month's schedule, call a supplier about a price rise, or rethink a menu item before another month of margin goes.

## What slows a close down

- Waiting on supplier statements
- Reconciling POS reports by hand
- Missing receipts for card purchases
- Payroll reports that arrive late

## How to get there

Send receipts as they happen, connect your POS, and agree a month-end date with your bookkeeper. Your bookkeeper handles the rest.


## Want a second pair of eyes?

Book a free month-end review. Your bookkeeper will walk through last month's numbers with you and show you where the time and money went. It takes about thirty minutes and there is nothing to install.
