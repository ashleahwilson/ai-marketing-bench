# A month-end checklist for restaurant owners

Month end goes faster when the same things happen in the same order every time. Here is the checklist we give new clients.

## In the last week of the month

- Send any receipts you are still holding
- Confirm supplier statements have arrived
- Schedule your inventory count for the last night of the month

## On the first day of the new month

- Count inventory, starting with your most expensive items
- Close out the POS for the month
- Send payroll reports if your bookkeeper doesn't receive them directly

## In the first week

- Answer your bookkeeper's questions in one sitting
- Review the P&L when it arrives: sales, prime cost, labour %
- Pick one number to improve next month

## Make it routine

Put these dates in your calendar for the year. A routine is what turns month end from a scramble into a meeting.
