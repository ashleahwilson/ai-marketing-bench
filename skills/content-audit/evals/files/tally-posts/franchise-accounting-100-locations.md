# Accounting for franchise groups with 100+ locations

Running the books for a franchise group with more than a hundred locations is a different job from running them for one restaurant.

## Consolidation at scale

At this size you need a consolidation system, an in-house controller and a close calendar shared by every location.

## Royalties and fund contributions

Royalty and marketing fund contributions should be booked by location and reconciled against franchisor statements every month.

## Audit readiness

Large groups should expect lender and franchisor audits. Keep supporting documents by location.
