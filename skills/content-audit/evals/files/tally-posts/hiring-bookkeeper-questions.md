# 10 questions to ask before hiring a restaurant bookkeeper

Hiring a restaurant bookkeeper is easier when you know what to ask. These ten questions separate someone who knows restaurants from someone who will learn on your books.

1. Have you worked in a restaurant or hospitality finance role?
2. How do you get sales data from my POS?
3. How do you handle tips and tip pooling?
4. When will I get my P&L each month?
5. Do you track prime cost?
6. Are sales tax filings included?
7. Will I have one named bookkeeper?
8. Is the price fixed or hourly?
9. What do you need from me each month?
10. What happens if I fall behind?

## What good answers sound like

Specific ones. "We connect to your POS and reconcile daily" is a good answer. "Send us a spreadsheet" is a warning sign.
