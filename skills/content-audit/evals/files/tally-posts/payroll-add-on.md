# Run payroll with your books: our new payroll add-on

Starting this month, Tallyhouse clients can add payroll to their plan for $59/month.

## What the payroll add-on does

Your bookkeeper runs payroll every two weeks, files remittances and keeps labour cost on your dashboard up to date without a separate export. Tips are pooled and paid out using the rules you set.

## Why we built it

Owners told us payroll was the last thing they still did by hand. Putting it next to the books means labour cost on your P&L matches what you actually paid.

## How to add it

Ask your bookkeeper to turn on the payroll add-on. It takes effect from your next pay period.


## Want a second pair of eyes?

Book a free month-end review. Your bookkeeper will walk through last month's numbers with you and show you where the time and money went. It takes about thirty minutes and there is nothing to install.
