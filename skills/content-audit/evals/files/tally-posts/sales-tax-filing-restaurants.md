# Sales tax filing for restaurants

Sales tax for a restaurant looks simple until you add delivery apps, gift cards and alcohol. Here is what to keep straight.

## What's taxable

Rules differ by province and state. Prepared food, alcohol and delivery fees are often treated differently, so check how your POS is set up for each item category.

## Delivery apps

Some delivery apps collect and remit tax on your behalf and some don't. Your bookkeeper needs each app's statements to avoid paying the same tax twice.

## Gift cards

Tax usually applies when a gift card is redeemed, not when it is sold. Make sure your POS reports them that way.
