# Tallyhouse vs a general bookkeeper

A general bookkeeper can keep a restaurant's books. The difference is what they already know and how your sales get into the books.

## Restaurant experience

Every Tallyhouse bookkeeper has worked in hospitality finance. You won't need to explain tip pooling, prime cost or why your food cost jumped after a holiday weekend.

## POS reconciliation

Daily sales from Toast, Square and Lightspeed are reconciled automatically. A general bookkeeper often works from exports you send at month end.

## What's included

Every Tallyhouse plan includes a monthly P&L, sales tax filings and a named bookkeeper. Ask a general bookkeeper which of these are extra.

## When a general bookkeeper is the better fit

If restaurants are a small part of a larger business, or you already have an in-house controller, a general bookkeeper may suit you better.
