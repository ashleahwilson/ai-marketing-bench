# What is prime cost? A restaurant owner's definition

Prime cost is the total of your cost of goods sold and your labour cost. Restaurant owners use it because it combines the two costs that move the most from week to week.

## The formula

Prime cost = cost of goods sold + total labour cost

To compare periods, divide it by total sales.

## Why it matters

It shows in one number whether your kitchen and your schedule are under control. If it rises, look at purchases and labour hours first.
