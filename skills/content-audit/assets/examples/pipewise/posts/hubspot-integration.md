# Forecasting from HubSpot deal data

Connect HubSpot once and Pipewise reads your deals, stages and close dates every night. Nothing to export. The integration is included on every plan.

## What it reads

Deal amount, stage, owner, close date and activity history.

## What it doesn't change

Pipewise never writes to HubSpot. Your reps keep working in HubSpot as they do today.
