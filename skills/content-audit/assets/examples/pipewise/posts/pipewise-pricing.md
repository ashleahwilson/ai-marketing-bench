# What Pipewise costs and why

Pipewise is $39 per rep per month, billed annually, with a minimum of 10 reps. HubSpot and Salesforce integrations are included on every plan.

## Why per rep

Forecasting gets more useful as more reps' deals feed it, so the price follows the size of the team.

## What you get

Every morning, your pipeline ranked by likelihood to close this month, and a forecast you can take to the board.

## Try it on your own data

Book a 30-minute forecast walkthrough with your own pipeline.
