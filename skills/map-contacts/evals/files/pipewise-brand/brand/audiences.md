## Personas

### Revenue leader
- Role: VP Sales or CRO [source: sales team, 2026-10-05 · confirmed]
- Measured on: forecast accuracy and quarter attainment [source: sales team, 2026-10-05 · confirmed]
- Pains: deals slip in the last two weeks of the quarter with no warning [source: win-loss interviews, 2026-09 · confirmed]
- Usually: economic buyer [source: sales team, 2026-10-05 · confirmed]

### RevOps lead
- Role: RevOps manager or sales operations lead [source: sales team, 2026-10-05 · confirmed]
- Measured on: CRM data quality and reporting turnaround [source: sales team, 2026-10-05 · confirmed]
- Pains: rebuilding the forecast spreadsheet every week [source: win-loss interviews, 2026-09 · confirmed]
- Usually: technical evaluator and day-to-day champion [source: sales team, 2026-10-05 · confirmed]

### Front-line sales manager
- Role: sales manager or team lead [source: sales team, 2026-10-05 · confirmed]
- Measured on: team attainment [source: sales team, 2026-10-05 · confirmed]
- Usually: user and influencer [source: sales team, 2026-10-05 · confirmed]

### Finance partner
