# About Tallyhouse

Founded in 2021 by Dev Patel, a former restaurant controller, and Hannah Ruiz, a CPA. We're a team of 14 based in Toronto, serving restaurants across Canada and the US.

We believe restaurant owners should spend their nights in the kitchen, not in spreadsheets.

Our bookkeepers have all worked in hospitality finance. We don't serve retail, e-commerce or franchises with more than 15 locations.
